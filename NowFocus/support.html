📱 About NowFocus

NowFocus is an ADHD-friendly productivity tool that helps you stay focused on your current task. It seamlessly integrates with macOS Notes, displaying your schedule across Mac and iOS devices through menu bar integration, widgets, and Live Activities.

🍎 Mac Integration

Menu bar display and floating window show your current task without distraction.

📱 iOS Widgets

Home Screen widgets and Lock Screen Live Activities keep you on track.

📝 Notes-Based

Plan in the familiar Notes app - no new system to learn.

☁️ iCloud Sync

Your tasks automatically sync across all your devices.

🚀 Getting Started

Mac Setup

  1. Create Notes Folder Open the Notes app and create a new folder named NowFocus Work Plan
  2. Create Weekly Plan Create a note with the format: 20251229-20260104 周计划 (YYYYMMDD-YYYYMMDD)
  3. Add Your Schedule Use this format in your note:
    10:00-12:00 Development work
    14:00-15:00 Team meeting
  4. Launch NowFocus The app appears in your menu bar. Click the icon to see your tasks.
  5. Grant Permissions When prompted, allow NowFocus to access Notes in System Preferences → Privacy & Security → Automation

iOS Setup

  1. Open NowFocus App Launch the app on your iPhone or iPad
  2. Sync Data Your tasks will automatically sync from Mac via iCloud
  3. Add Widgets Long press on Home Screen → Add Widget → NowFocus
  4. Enable Live Activities Live Activities appear automatically on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island

Weekly Plan Structure

Create a note with the following structure:

## 本周计划

| 大目标 | 小目标 | 交付 |
|--------|--------|------|
| Complete Feature A | Design | Done |
| Complete Feature A | Development | |

## 周四: Task Manager

**今日目标**
- Complete core functionality
- Testing and validation

| 时间段 | 任务 | 交付 |
|--------|------|------|

Key Points

  • Time Format: Use HH:MM-HH:MM format
  • Completion: Add any text in the "交付" (Delivery) column to mark as done
  • Daily Section: Start with day of week: 周X: or M/D 周X
  • Today's Goals: Use bullet points with -

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why does NowFocus need access to Notes?

NowFocus reads your task schedule from the Notes app to display your current tasks. This integration keeps your workflow simple - plan in Notes, see tasks everywhere. The app only reads from the "NowFocus Work Plan" folder and never modifies or deletes your notes.

Q: How do I grant Notes permission?

Go to System Preferences → Privacy & Security → Automation, find NowFocus, and check the box next to Notes. You can revoke this permission at any time.

Q: My tasks aren't showing on iOS. What should I do?

Make sure: (1) The Mac app is running and has synced data, (2) You're signed into the same iCloud account on both devices, (3) iCloud Drive is enabled in System Preferences.

Q: How often do widgets update?

iOS widgets refresh approximately every 15 minutes (system limitation). The Mac menu bar updates every minute. You can manually refresh by clicking the "Refresh" button in the Mac app.

Q: Can I use NowFocus without iCloud?

The Mac app works independently without iCloud. However, iOS sync requires iCloud to transfer task data between devices.

Q: How do I mark tasks as completed?

In your Notes, add any text in the "交付" (Delivery) column of the task table. For example: "done", "completed", or a brief description of what was delivered.

Q: What happens if I don't have any tasks scheduled?

The Mac app will show "No Task" in the menu bar and send a notification (if enabled) to remind you to plan your day.

Q: Is my data private?

Yes! NowFocus processes all data locally on your devices. Task data syncs through your personal iCloud account. We do not collect, store, or transmit any data to external servers. Read our full Privacy Policy.

Q: Can I customize the notification settings?

Notification preferences can be managed in System Preferences → Notifications → NowFocus. You can choose banner style, sounds, and whether notifications appear on Lock Screen.

🔧 Troubleshooting

Mac App Issues

App doesn't appear in menu bar

Check if the app is running in Activity Monitor. Try quitting and relaunching. Make sure LSUIElement is properly configured (this is automatic - contact support if issue persists).

Can't read Notes / Permission denied

Go to System Preferences → Privacy & Security → Automation, find NowFocus, and ensure Notes is checked. If the issue persists, try removing and re-granting the permission.

Floating window doesn't show

Click the menu bar icon and select "Show Desktop Widget". If it still doesn't appear, try quitting and restarting the app.

iOS App Issues

Widgets not updating

Remove and re-add the widget. Ensure the Mac app is running and syncing. Check iCloud Drive status in Settings. Widget updates are controlled by iOS and may take up to 15 minutes.

Live Activities not appearing

Check that Live Activities are enabled: Settings → NowFocus → Live Activities. Also verify Focus mode settings aren't blocking Live Activities.

🎯 Tips for ADHD Users

Time Blocking

Schedule specific time blocks for tasks. Seeing exact times helps reduce decision fatigue.

Buffer Time

Add 15-30 minute buffers between tasks to account for transitions and breaks.

Daily Goals

List 2-3 key goals each day. Don't overwhelm yourself with too many priorities.

Weekly Review

Check your weekly goals progress. Adjust expectations based on what's realistic.

Visual Reminders

Keep the floating window visible on your desktop for constant visual cues.

Delivery Notes

Writing what you accomplished (in the 交付 column) provides positive reinforcement.
