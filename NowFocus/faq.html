❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why does NowFocus need access to Notes?

NowFocus reads your task schedule from the Notes app to display your current tasks. This integration keeps your workflow simple - plan in Notes, see tasks everywhere. The app only reads from the "NowFocus Work Plan" folder and never modifies or deletes your notes.

Q: How do I grant Notes permission?

Go to System Preferences → Privacy & Security → Automation, find NowFocus, and check the box next to Notes. You can revoke this permission at any time.

Q: My tasks aren't showing on iOS. What should I do?

Make sure: (1) The Mac app is running and has synced data, (2) You're signed into the same iCloud account on both devices, (3) iCloud Drive is enabled in System Preferences.

Q: How often do widgets update?

iOS widgets refresh approximately every 15 minutes (system limitation). The Mac menu bar updates every minute. You can manually refresh by clicking the "Refresh" button in the Mac app.

Q: Can I use NowFocus without iCloud?

The Mac app works independently without iCloud. However, iOS sync requires iCloud to transfer task data between devices.

Q: How do I mark tasks as completed?

In your Notes, add any text in the "交付" (Delivery) column of the task table. For example: "done", "completed", or a brief description of what was delivered.

Q: What happens if I don't have any tasks scheduled?

The Mac app will show "No Task" in the menu bar and send a notification (if enabled) to remind you to plan your day.

Q: Is my data private?

Yes! NowFocus processes all data locally on your devices. Task data syncs through your personal iCloud account. We do not collect, store, or transmit any data to external servers. Read our full Privacy Policy.

Q: Can I customize the notification settings?

Notification preferences can be managed in System Preferences → Notifications → NowFocus. You can choose banner style, sounds, and whether notifications appear on Lock Screen.
